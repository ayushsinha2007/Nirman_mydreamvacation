ABOUT THE DESTINATION

Where Faith Meets the Himalayas

Kedarnath is a revered pilgrimage destination in the Rudraprayag district of Uttarakhand, India. Located in the Garhwal Himalayas, it is home to the historic Kedarnath Temple, dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Surrounded by snow-covered peaks, rivers, valleys, and mountain trails, Kedarnath offers more than a trip—it is a peaceful and memorable journey through nature, culture, and devotion.

EXPLORE KEDARNATH

Unforgettable Highlights

Kedarnath Temple

Visit the sacred temple of Lord Shiva, set in a spectacular Himalayan valley and surrounded by dramatic mountain peaks.

The Himalayan Trek

Experience a scenic mountain journey from Gaurikund towards Kedarnath, passing rivers, forests, and impressive views.

Vasuki Tal

Discover the beauty of a high-altitude glacial lake surrounded by the rugged landscapes of the Himalayas.

Bhairavnath Temple

Visit the temple of Bhairavnath Ji, regarded as the guardian deity of Kedarnath, and enjoy panoramic views over Kedarnath Valley.

SUGGESTED ITINERARY

A 4-Day Kedarnath Journey

01

Reach Sonprayag or Gaurikund

Arrive at Sonprayag or Gaurikund, settle in, enjoy the mountain surroundings, and prepare for the journey to Kedarnath.

02

Begin the Journey to Kedarnath

Start the route from Gaurikund towards Kedarnath. Take your time, stay hydrated, and enjoy the changing mountain landscape.

03

Temple Darshan and Exploration

Visit Kedarnath Temple, spend time in the peaceful surroundings, and explore nearby viewpoints as conditions allow.

04

Return with Memories

Begin your return journey to Gaurikund and carry home memories of the mountains, the temple, and the unique Kedarnath experience.

TRAVEL RESPONSIBLY

Useful Travel Tips

  • Check weather conditions and official travel updates before starting your journey.
  • Carry warm layers, rain protection, comfortable walking shoes, and essential medicines.
  • Stay hydrated and give yourself time to adjust to the mountain altitude.
  • Respect local culture, temple rules, fellow pilgrims, and the natural environment.
  • Carry back all waste and help keep the Himalayas clean.
